## Printed UK passport photo requirements

For a paper application, you need **2 identical printed photos**, each
**35 mm wide and 45 mm tall**. The distance from your chin to the crown of your
head must be **29 mm to 34 mm**. The measurements in the table above refer to
this printed format.

Photos must be clear, in colour, unaltered by computer software and printed to
a professional standard on plain white photographic paper without a border.
They must have been taken in the last month, even if your appearance has not
changed. Follow the [official printed photo rules](https://www.gov.uk/photos-for-passports/photo-requirements)
for the full requirements.

## Digital photo requirements for an online application

An online application needs a digital photo, not a scan of the printed format.
The [official digital photo rules](https://www.gov.uk/photos-for-passports/rules-for-digital-photos)
require a photo that is:

- At least **600 pixels wide and 750 pixels tall**.
- At least **50 KB** and no more than **10 MB**.
- Clear, in focus, in colour and **unaltered by computer software**.
- Taken against a plain, light-coloured background with no shadows.

If you take the photo on your own device, include your head, shoulders and upper
body. **Do not crop it yourself**; the application service crops it for you.
You can also obtain a digital photo code from a photo booth or shop.

## Background, clothing and glasses

Printed photos need a plain cream or light grey background. Digital photos need
a plain light-coloured background. Both need clear contrast and no shadows on
your face or behind you. Use an appropriate background when taking the photo;
software replacement does not satisfy the unaltered-photo rule.

Face forward with your eyes visible and your mouth closed. Keep hair and
clothing away from your face. Head coverings are allowed only for religious or
medical reasons. A shirt that contrasts with the background can help, but
GOV.UK does not prescribe a shirt colour.

Avoid glasses unless you have to wear them. If you do, your eyes must remain
visible without tinted lenses, frame obstruction, glare or shadows. Children
under 6 do not need a plain expression or to look directly at the camera;
children under 1 do not need their eyes open. See the official guidance for
the other rules for babies and children.

## What this preview can and cannot provide

This tool produces a **413 x 531 pixel** image, corresponding to 35 x 45 mm at
300 DPI, and a 4 x 6 inch print sheet. These downloads are previews for checking
framing and layout, **not photos to submit for a UK passport**.

The tool replaces the background, while both printed and digital UK passport
photos must be unaltered by computer software. The single-image export is also
smaller than the digital minimum. Increasing its DPI or enlarging it would not
resolve the alteration issue. Use an unaltered original following GOV.UK
guidance, or obtain photos from a photo booth or shop. Photos opened here stay
on your device.
